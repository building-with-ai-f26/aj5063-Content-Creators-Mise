## 1. Who is it for?
*Name a specific person or role you have direct access to (not a broad demographic). Who will actually test and use this before Demo Day?*

- **Target user:** A friend who runs an Instagram food account, posting about 3x/week, doing everything herself (cooking, filming, writing, editing).
- **How I will reach them to test it:** Direct message.

## 2. What job does it do?
*Describe the concrete recurring problem from work, school, or life, and what "done" looks like for the user.*

- **Current painful workflow (before)** *(confirmed with her by DM)*:
  1. Develops and cooks the recipe, jotting rough notes on ingredients and steps.
  2. Films the cooking process on her phone.
  3. Writes a script/voiceover for the Reel, condensing the recipe into 30–60 seconds.
  4. Edits the video and adds on-screen text.
  5. Writes a separate Instagram caption: a hook, a condensed ingredient list and steps, and hashtags.
  6. If she reposts to TikTok or a blog, she rewrites everything again in that platform's format.

  **Pain:** Rewriting the same recipe 3–4 times in different formats and lengths — repetitive, eats time she'd rather spend cooking and filming, and adds up fast at 3 posts a week.
- **New workflow with this product (after):**
  1. Cooks the recipe and jots rough notes, same as before.
  2. Pastes those notes into Mise and picks her platforms (Reel script + caption now; TikTok or blog later).
  3. Mise returns a ready-to-use package: a 30–60 second video script with a hook, and an Instagram caption with a condensed ingredient list, steps, and hashtags.
  4. Reviews and tweaks the wording so it sounds like her, then films using the script as her guide.
  5. Edits the video and posts, copying the caption straight from Mise.
- **Why this is useful** *(used weekly / saves real time / worth paying for)*: She writes the recipe once instead of 3–4 times. The two slowest, most repetitive steps of her workflow (script writing and caption writing) go from "write from scratch" to "quick edit."
